CHAPTER ONE · FOR YOUR HEART

For Your Heart

For peace, happiness and all the little things I ask Allah to take care of.

✧

01

For her peace

Ya Allah, when her mind feels heavy, grant her a peace that reaches places words cannot.

02

For her difficult days

Ya Allah, make the days that feel difficult a little easier for her. And remind her that Your mercy is always near.

03

For the things she keeps inside

Ya Allah, You know the things she never says. You know every worry she carries quietly. Please take care of what she cannot explain.

04

For her smile

Ya Allah, keep goodness in her life, give her countless reasons to be grateful, and never let the hardships of this world take away her hope.

05

For her heart

Ya Allah, keep her heart close to You. Let her always find comfort in remembering You.

CHAPTER ONE · FOR YOUR HEART

And a little more...

Some prayers are too important to leave unsaid.

✧

06

For her happiness

Ya Allah, bless her with happiness from places she never expected, and goodness better than anything she could have imagined.

07

For her worries

Ya Allah, replace her worries with ease, her uncertainty with trust in You, and her fears with strength.

08

For her path ahead

Ya Allah, guide her towards what is best for her, keep her away from what may harm her, and put barakah in every step she takes.

09

For her future

Ya Allah, write beautiful goodness for her in this life and the next. Give her what is good, at the time that is best.

10

For her akhirah

Ya Allah, forgive her, have mercy on her, accept her good deeds, and grant her Jannatul Firdaus.

✧

May Allah take care of every little thing her heart holds.

CHAPTER TWO · FOR YOUR IMAAN

For Your Imaan

For her faith, her guidance, and her connection with Allah.

✧

01. For her imaan

Ya Allah, strengthen her imaan with every passing day. Let her faith remain firm through every season of her life.

02. For her connection with You

Ya Allah, bring her closer to You in ways that make her heart feel at peace. Never let her feel distant from Your mercy.

03. For her salah

Ya Allah, make salah a comfort for her, not a burden. Let her find peace in every sujood and strength in every prayer.

04. For her guidance

Ya Allah, guide her in every decision she makes. When the path ahead feels unclear, lead her towards what pleases You.

05. For her love of the Qur'an

Ya Allah, place a deep love for the Qur'an in her heart. Let its words guide her choices, comfort her soul, and remain with her throughout her life.

CHAPTER TWO · FOR YOUR IMAAN

A Faith That Stays

For the strength to remain close to the One who created her.

✧

06. For her steadfastness

Ya Allah, keep her firm upon Your deen, even when the world makes it difficult. Let her never lose her way.

07. For her repentance

Ya Allah, whenever she makes a mistake, guide her back to You. Make returning to You easy for her, and never let her lose hope in Your forgiveness.

08. For her inner strength

Ya Allah, give her the strength to choose what pleases You, even when it is not easy. Protect her from the things that slowly pull her away from You.

09. For her sincerity

Ya Allah, purify her intentions. Let the good she does be for Your sake, even when nobody sees it or knows about it.

10. For her akhirah

Ya Allah, make her among those who meet You with a heart full of imaan. Forgive her, have mercy on her, and grant her the highest place in Jannatul Firdaus.

✧

AMEEN, YA RABB.

May her faith keep growing, may her heart keep returning to Allah, and may she always find her way back to Him.

CHAPTER 03

For Your Protection

May Allah's protection always surround her.

01. For her protection

Ya Allah, protect her wherever she goes, wherever she stays, and wherever life takes her. Let Your protection surround her in ways she may never even realise.

02. For what she cannot see

Ya Allah, protect her from every harm she cannot see coming. Keep her safe from what is hidden, unknown, and beyond her understanding.

03. For the people around her

Ya Allah, surround her with people whose presence brings goodness into her life. Keep her away from those who may deceive her, hurt her, or lead her away from what is right.

04. For her journeys

Ya Allah, watch over her every journey. Wherever she travels, bring her there safely and return her home in peace.

05. For her health

Ya Allah, bless her with good health, protect her from illness, and grant her strength and well-being. Let her body and mind be under Your care.

CHAPTER 03

Always Under Your Care

For the moments she needs Your protection most.

06. For her in difficult moments

Ya Allah, when she faces something beyond her strength, be her protection and make a way through it that she never expected.

07. For her from jealousy and ill intentions

Ya Allah, protect her from envy, ill intentions, and the harm of those who do not wish goodness for her. Keep her surrounded by Your mercy.

08. For her peace of mind

Ya Allah, protect her mind from overwhelming worries and her heart from unnecessary fear. Grant her the peace that comes from trusting You.

09. For her when she is alone

Ya Allah, when nobody is beside her, remind her that she is never beyond Your care. Watch over her in every quiet moment.

10. For her in this life and the next

Ya Allah, protect her in this world and the Hereafter. Keep her safe from what displeases You, forgive her shortcomings, and grant her a place in Jannatul Firdaus.

May Allah protect her in places your duas cannot reach, and in moments your eyes cannot see.

AMEEN, YA RABB.

CHAPTER FOUR · FOR YOUR FUTURE

For Your Future

For the dreams she carries and the life waiting ahead.

✧

01. For her dreams

Ya Allah, if the dreams she carries are good for her, bring them closer to her. And bless her with something even better than what she hopes for.

02. For her decisions

Ya Allah, guide her towards the right decisions. When she stands between different paths, lead her towards the one that brings goodness into her life and pleasure to You.

03. For her opportunities

Ya Allah, open doors of goodness for her that she never imagined. Place opportunities in her path that bring growth, happiness, and barakah.

04. For her success

Ya Allah, grant her success in the things she works hard for. Let her efforts bring beautiful results, and never let success take her heart away from You.

05. For what is written for her

Ya Allah, make her content with what You have written for her. When something is not meant for her, give her the strength to let it go and the trust to believe in Your plans.

CHAPTER FOUR · FOR YOUR FUTURE

A Beautiful Life Ahead

For the blessings, people, and moments that are yet to come.

✧

06. For her education and knowledge

Ya Allah, increase her in beneficial knowledge. Make learning easy for her, bless her efforts, and let what she learns become a source of goodness in her life.

07. For her provisions

Ya Allah, grant her halal rizq, abundant barakah, and a life free from unnecessary hardship. Never let her worry about what You have already promised to provide.

08. For the people she will meet

Ya Allah, bring sincere and righteous people into her life. Surround her with those who encourage her towards goodness, respect her, and remind her of You.

09. For the happiness she has yet to experience

Ya Allah, fill the chapters of her life that she has not reached yet with unexpected blessings, beautiful moments, and reasons to thank You.

10. For her life in this world and the next

Ya Allah, grant her a beautiful life filled with goodness, but never let this world become more important to her than the Hereafter. Make her final destination Jannatul Firdaus.

✧

AMEEN, YA RABB.

May the future hold more goodness for her than she has ever imagined, and may every step bring her closer to what is best in this life and the next.

SOMETHING I LEAVE TO ALLAH

If It Is Written...

A prayer for two hearts, and whatever Allah has written for them.

Ya Allah, You know what is in my heart. You know what I quietly wish for, what I hope for, and what I cannot put into words. You know her, You know me, and You know the future that neither of us can see.

Ya Allah, if there is goodness in our naseeb together, if being part of each other's lives is good for our deen, our dunya, and our akhirah, then make a beautiful way for us. Remove the difficulties that can be removed, guide our steps towards what pleases You, and place barakah in whatever You write for us.

And Ya Allah, if our paths seem distant, if circumstances stand between us, or if things appear difficult, I ask You to make a way if there is goodness in it. You are the One who can bring ease where there is difficulty, open doors nobody else can open, and bring about what seems impossible to us.

Ya Allah, if being together can be made good for us, then write us into each other's naseeb. Make what seems distant come closer, make what seems difficult easier, and let whatever future You grant us be built upon imaan, honesty, respect, patience, and Your blessings.

Protect our hearts and our intentions. Never let our feelings take us away from You. Keep us on the path that is halal and pleasing to You. And if Your plan for us is different, then give us the wisdom to accept it and guide us towards whatever is better.

I am asking You, Ya Rabb, because what is beyond my ability is never beyond Yours. I cannot see tomorrow, but You already know it. I cannot control what is written, but I can place my hopes and my duas in Your hands.

I don't know what tomorrow holds, Ya Allah. I don't know what You have written in our naseeb.

But if there is goodness in us being together, then write us for each other. Make a way for us, make it beautiful, and place Your blessings in it.

And above everything, let Your decision be better than anything I have ever imagined.

AMEEN, YA RABB.
